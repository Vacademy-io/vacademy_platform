## `crm_reports`

**CRM reports: view** · Read only

See CRM reports — lead sources and campaigns, the funnel, counsellor performance and workload, calling and AI calling, follow-up ageing, SLA breaches, meetings and revenue — each figure with its date range and how it is counted. Each user sees the figures for the leads they can see.

| Action | What it does | Changes data | How it applies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `overview` | Leads in a window: totals, by status, source and tier, daily trend. | No | — |
| `sources` | Per lead source: leads, connected, converted; aliases with the raw keys counted. | No | — |
| `campaigns` | Per lead list: leads, converted, lost and unassigned in the window. | No | — |
| `funnel` | Per status: leads that entered it in the window and leads in it now. | No | — |
| `status_changes` | Lead status changes per person who made them. | No | — |
| `counsellors` | Per counsellor: leads, conversions, open leads, calls (AI / human) and rating. | No | — |
| `workload` | Per owner now: open leads, new this week, open and overdue follow-ups. | No | — |
| `targets` | Workbench targets vs completed per counsellor for a week, month or custom period. | No | — |
| `calling` | Dials, connected calls and talk time per day and counsellor, split AI vs human. | No | — |
| `ai_calling` | AI calls by agent and outcome, connect rate, follow-ups asked; credits for admins. | No | — |
| `followup_aging` | Open follow-ups by age (due today, overdue 1-3 / 4-7 / 8+ days) per counsellor. | No | — |
| `sla_breaches` | Live leads past their first-response deadline, by owner, list and source. | No | — |

| `utm` | UTM campaign attribution: people and enrolments per source, medium and campaign. *(institute admins only)* | No | — |

| `custom` | An ad-hoc report: leads and activity grouped by up to two dimensions (source, list, owner, status, tier, date, a form field), with chosen metrics, filters and totals. | No | — |

</ParamField>

</ParamField>
<ParamField path="filters" type="object">

</ParamField>
